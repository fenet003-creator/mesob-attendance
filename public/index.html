About MESOB Center

መሶብ ማዕከል —
Ethiopia's Comprehensive
Government Services Hub

MESOB Center is a one-stop platform consolidating Ethiopian government services — from trade and business registration to immigration, customs, agriculture, and more. Our internship platform extends this mission by building the next generation of professionals who will serve Ethiopia's growing institutions.

Trade & Business Services

Business name registration, license amendments, and organization approval through the Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration (MOTRI). Fees from 27–127 birr.

motri.gov.et

Immigration & Identity

Fayda National ID registration, Ethiopian Origin ID (Yellow Card), passport services, and visa processing through ICS. ID booking is free.

ics.gov.et

Customs & Trade

Customs duty exemptions for spare parts, construction materials, and capital goods. Bonded warehouse licenses from 1,250 ETB/year.

Customs Authority

Agriculture & Environment

Pesticide, fertilizer, and seed competency certificates through EAA. Environmental licenses and NON-GMO certificates through EPA.

eaa.gov.et

Food & Drug Authority

Competency certificates for medicine and medical device importers and distributors. Warehouse licenses for pharmaceutical storage.

efda.gov.et

Transport & Logistics

Commercial road transport operator licenses, freight competency certifications, and border entry permit renewals through MoTL.

motl.gov.et
Platform Capabilities

Built for Ethiopia's Growing Institutions

Comprehensive internship management designed for efficiency, accountability, and transparency across government offices.

Real-Time Attendance

Track check-ins and check-outs with configurable thresholds for Present (before 8:15), Late (8:15–8:30), and Absent statuses.

Application Management

Review, approve, or reject internship applications with full audit trail and document tracking.

Supervisor Oversight

Assign supervisors to interns, monitor activity, and manage mentorship across departments.

Placement Tracking

Manage internship placements across departments with capacity tracking and status updates.

Analytics & Reports

Generate operational reports on attendance trends, application statistics, and supervisor workload.

Announcements

Send targeted notifications to interns, supervisors, or the entire organization with priority levels.
